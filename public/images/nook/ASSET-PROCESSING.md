# Nook delivery derivatives

No generative tools or AI image processing were used for these assets.

## `mog.webp`

- Source: `../moogle-magic.png` (925 × 831 pixels), unchanged in the repository.
- Artwork source and publisher details: [../SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md), entry
  `moogle-magic.png`.
- Original publisher page:
  https://www.jp.square-enix.com/itastDQFF30th/about/moogle.html
- Original publisher image:
  https://www.jp.square-enix.com/itastDQFF30th/about/_img/set/moogle/2d/chara_pic.png
- Credit: © SQUARE ENIX CO., LTD. All Rights Reserved.

The derivative removes only fully transparent exterior margins, preserving every
source pixel whose alpha is greater than zero. The exact alpha bounds are left
238, top 66, width 417, height 548. This uses a scan of the alpha channel and an
exact rectangular Sharp extraction, rather than a color-based trim that might
remove faint illustrated pixels. No scale, recolor, retouch, or illustration edit
was applied. Sharp encoded the result as WebP with `lossless: true, effort: 6`.

Final dimensions are 417 × 548 pixels; file size is 75,484 bytes. The original and
cropped result were inspected in the image viewer, including the derivative
composited over a pale cream matte (`#f9eadf`) to check its edges and alpha.

## `ivy.webp`

See [BOTANICAL-SOURCES.md](BOTANICAL-SOURCES.md) for the historical public-domain
source and exact deterministic extraction process. Its delivery derivative is
600 × 700 pixels, quality 90 with alpha quality 100, 129,060 bytes. The original
`ivy-source.jpg` remains unchanged.
