# Nook window scenery

These are original in-game screenshots published by Square Enix in its 2021
FINAL FANTASY XIV Artwork Exhibition. They are not AI-generated art. The downloaded
JPEG files are unchanged from the publisher's assets.

## Day window

- Local file: `window-day.jpg`
- Scene: The Brume, Ishgard; snowy castle architecture in daylight.
- Publisher: Square Enix.
- Original size: 960 × 1920 pixels.
- Publisher gallery: https://www.finalfantasyxiv.com/promotion/genso-zekkei/zekkei/02/
- Original asset: https://www.finalfantasyxiv.com/promotion/genso-zekkei/static/8e2cd2f33fae929515306acf64af1695/4384d/06_coerthas_01.jpg

## Night window

- Local file: `window-night.jpg`
- Scene: Ishgard skyline above the clouds at blue twilight. The filename identifies
  its use in the site's dark appearance; the screenshot itself is a twilight view.
- Publisher: Square Enix.
- Original size: 1920 × 963 pixels.
- Publisher gallery: https://www.finalfantasyxiv.com/promotion/genso-zekkei/zekkei/02/
- Original asset: https://www.finalfantasyxiv.com/promotion/genso-zekkei/static/318d140a200d4d4f933a2a70bc7b3b3c/6baa6/06_coerthas_02.jpg
