# MogTome copy

Write as an FC member talking to another member: friendly, playful, and easy
to understand. Keep the cozy scrapbook and moogle personality; soften excess.

## What to aim for

- Use everyday words and contractions. Make actions and next steps clear.
- Welcome people warmly. Home and scrapbook language fit when natural.
- Keep character moments: a moogle’s “kupo,” a playful loading message, or a
  joke that fits what’s happening.
- Match the moment. Loading and empty states can be lighthearted; errors,
  account security, and recovery instructions should be calm and precise.
- Be specific to Kupo Life. Don’t invent events, habits, or promises.
- Keep Kupo Life, Members, Chronicle, and official FFXIV rank names.
- Refer to people as members and to the group as the FC or Free Company.
- Preserve member-written biographies and other user content.

## What to cut

- Repeated sentimental slogans, stacked cute phrases, and constant catchphrases.
- Hype when the sentence just means “view,” “read,” or “join.”
- Stock transitions, artificial groups of three, and unnecessary “not just X,
  but Y” framing.
- Forced jokes or baby talk that obscure a task.

There are no word bans or quotas. “Kupo,” exclamation marks, and tildes can
work. Read aloud: would someone in the FC actually say this?

## Examples

| Copy                                           | Guidance                            |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Rounding everyone up, kupo...                  | Keep: explicitly liked by the user. |
| Welcome home, kupo                             | A fitting warm welcome.             |
| Same friends. New stories. Always home, kupo ♡ | Trim: “See you in game, kupo.”      |
| Couldn’t save your bio. Try again.             | Keep errors clear and actionable.   |

## Reading

- [Mailchimp: Voice and tone](https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/voice-and-tone/):
  prioritize clarity and avoid forced humor or fluffy metaphors.
- [GOV.UK: Use clear language](https://guidance.publishing.service.gov.uk/writing-to-gov-uk-standards/writing-guidelines/clear-language/):
  use familiar words and concrete explanations.
- [Grammarly: Common words and phrases in AI-generated text](https://www.grammarly.com/blog/ai/common-ai-words/):
  watch for repetitive structures, stock phrases, and generic claims.

These sources inform clarity; the voice choices are our application to
MogTome and the user’s preference for personality.
